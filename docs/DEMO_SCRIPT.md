# OnGround IPIS — Live Hackathon Presentation & Demo Script

**Problem Statement 26122:** Automated Infrastructure Data Capture & Schedule-Linking  
**Estimated Time:** 3 – 5 Minutes

---

## Part 1: The Hook & Pain Point (45 Seconds)

> **Speaker:**
> "In mega EPC infrastructure projects—refineries, highways, power plants—contractors submit hundreds of unstructured daily progress reports in PDFs, shift log TXTs, and spreadsheets.
> 
> Today, planners spend **15 to 20 hours every single week** manually reading these reports and searching through thousands of Primavera P6 WBS activities to link physical progress.
> 
> Human error leads to missed schedule delays, ghost progress, and multi-crore cost overruns.
> 
> We built **OnGround — Infrastructure Progress Intelligence System (IPIS)** to turn raw daily contractor logs into verified, semantically linked Primavera schedule updates in seconds."

## Part 5: Anticipated Judges' Questions & Technical Answers

| Question | Winning Technical Answer |
|---|---|
| **"Why not use LLM for matching too?"** | *"LLM matching is slow, non-deterministic, and costly. We use local `sentence-transformers` embeddings ($0.70 \times \text{sim} + 0.20 \times \text{conf} + 0.10 \times \text{date} - \text{penalty}$) which is deterministic, free, offline-capable, and runs in milliseconds."* |
| **"How do you prevent hallucinated confidence?"** | *"Our confidence scores are never generated by LLMs. They are deterministically computed server-side in Python based on field completeness (discipline, ISO dates, location references)."* |
| **"Can it work without internet?"** | *"Yes! OnGround has a full zero-cloud local fallback mode with local embeddings and offline extraction resilience."* |
